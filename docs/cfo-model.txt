Verbatim — unit economics & runway

Verbatim is an AI research agent that verifies every sentence it writes against its source and labels anything it can't verify. It sells monthly subscriptions (Free / Solo $49 / Team $199 / Enterprise custom).

How to read this page: the three scenarios change only the number of paying customers at month 12 (Downside 60, Base 250, Upside 900, all starting from 250 today). Price and cost per report are identical in every scenario, so gross margin is identical too — the scenarios differ in scale, burn and runway.

Headline: each paying customer earns ~$239/month and costs ~$40/month to serve (≈$9 inference + ≈$31 infrastructure), so gross margin is ~83%. Against $60k/month of operating costs, Verbatim breaks even at ~301 paying customers. Unit economics aren't the risk; distribution is.

Measured vs estimated: model cost per report is measured at $0.007 but the model uses a deliberately rounded-up $0.02 assumption. Enterprise price ($1,500/mo), Enterprise usage (2,000 reports/mo), full use of plan allowances, and 3 free users per paying customer are estimates. Cash ($500k) and opex ($60k/mo) are planning assumptions. All inputs live on the Verbatim Assumptions page.
